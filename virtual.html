⚙️ How this generator works

1 The bookmaker pre-calculates the odds. Each team carries attack / defence ratings (fixed per season). For every fixture: home_xg = ATK_home × DEF_away (and symmetrically for the away side), the exact Poisson score matrix gives every fair probability — prob_home_win = Σ Poisson(x; home_xg) · Poisson(y; away_xg) for x>y — and true_odds = 1 / probability. The 1X2 prices are true_odds × uniform: the vig wobbles ±3% around 6%, and on roughly 1 match in 5 the book deliberately misprices ONE line above true odds — a value edge, findable from the ratings tab. Derived markets carry flat margins; all prices are rounded before anyone can bet.

2 The RNG draws the score from the same xGs. Two independent streams sample each side's goals directly — score = Poisson(home_xg) : Poisson(away_xg), exactly the expected goals the prices were computed from — and a third places goal minutes (each goal lands before half-time with probability 0.45, matching how the 1st-half prices are computed). All markets settle from that one drawn score.

3 Commit & reveal. Each league-round publishes SHA-256 of its seed before the countdown ends; the seed is revealed after the draw and any round can be re-verified from it.

Format mimics the classic simulated-virtuals lobby: country leagues, 3-letter team codes, 10 matches per round, staggered clocks. Team codes are generic club abbreviations for simulation only.
