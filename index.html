👁 Monitor engine

The monitor learns from results. It reads five signals for every match: team form, odds movement (rising or dropping), expected goals, table position and attack / defence strength. When a match finishes, the engine files the real outcome under that exact pattern of signals.

How it works

1  Watch — every finished match is turned into a pattern: favourite strength · odds move · xG edge · table · form.
2  Remember — the final outcome is stored against that pattern, on this device.
3  Repeat — when today’s match shows the same pattern, the engine predicts the previous outcome and shows it under the MONITOR badge. One tap adds the call to your slip.

Memory, not magic: a MONITOR badge means this exact pattern happened before and finished that way. The green and red dots below show how often those calls have been right for you.

How it works

Each team's scoring rate is estimated from past meetings (or recent form where head-to-head is not offered), then shrunk toward that sport's average so a small sample can't produce a wild number. Those rates feed a Poisson model that produces a probability for every market.

Every sport uses its own scale. A football side averaging 1.4 goals and a basketball side averaging 84 points are both "normal" — the model holds separate averages and High/Low cut-offs per sport, so profiles and totals lines always make sense for the game you're looking at.

Scoring profile rates each side High, Mid or Low, then flags the matchup: High v High leans towards a high-scoring game, Low v Low towards a tight one, and a mismatch often means one-sided.

Injury level is football only — the other sports' APIs do not publish an injuries endpoint, so those legs show "Not offered for this sport" rather than a guess.

Confidence reflects how much real history the estimate is built on. Low confidence pulls ratings toward neutral.
